8 of 18 YC B2B startups hiring a marketer right now have no llms.txt

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 2026

A company that just opened a marketing role has budget and a pipeline goal, which looks like a Maintouch buyer. I took the 18 B2B startups hiring for marketing or growth on YC's job board and checked their sites for AI-search basics. 8 have no llms.txt, and in 2 of those the newest dated blog page in the sitemap is more than 90 days old. Rebill is hiring a Head of Marketing, B2B Payments and its newest dated blog page is from July 20.

How it works

  • Pull every open marketing and growth role from YC's public job board and keep the B2B companies.
  • For each site, read robots.txt and sitemap.xml, count blog pages with a lastmod date in the last 90 days, and check for /llms.txt.
  • Rank companies with no llms.txt and no recent blog first, so a seller opens with what is missing on their own site.

What I'd improve next: Add an AEO check that asks ChatGPT and Perplexity the buyer's top category questions and records whether the company is cited, then rerun weekly so a new marketing hire or a new blog post changes the order.

Avante sells to enterprise benefits teams. One of the hardest jobs they get is an acquisition: two sets of plans, vendors and contracts to compare, and a lot of worried employees. These 10 U.S. employers announced or closed deals in November. CrowdStrike is first because the company it is buying, Zscaler, is already an Avante customer.

Method: pulled November "completes acquisition" and "to acquire" news from Google News, kept U.S. employers buying companies with real workforces, and scored on deal size, how soon the close is, and how different the two workforces look. I did not check these against your current customers or broker partners.

How it was built

  • Start with who actually buys from you, based on your own job post, site, and press, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days, plus a second signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on how recent the event is, how closely the account matches your buyer, and how usable it is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the saved source text. Nothing here is invented.

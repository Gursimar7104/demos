Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 24, 2026, on real public data

Wealth.com wins when a growing firm picks one estate planning system for all its advisors, the way &Partners did for 100+ advisors in September. The best time to have that conversation is right after a firm buys other practices and has to bring new advisors and their client files onto one process. These are acquirers with dated deals in the last ~60 days.

Method: pulled October 2026 RIA deals from AdvizorPro (built from SEC ADV-W filings) and November deal news from InvestmentNews and Dealroom, kept the repeat or well-funded acquirers, and wrote a first line from the specific deal. I did not check these against your current customer list, so some may already be on Wealth.com.

How it was built

  • Start with who actually buys from you, based on your own job post, site, and press, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days, plus a second signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on how recent the event is, how closely the account matches your buyer, and how usable it is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the saved source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Cross-check against your Salesforce customers and open opps first, then add a second signal per firm (a new Head of Planning hire, or a job post that mentions estate or tax planning) and route by advisor headcount.

PointOne sells to law firm partners, CFOs and ops leaders. Firms lose the most billable time during big changes: a merger, a group lateral, or a new office where nobody's habits are set yet. Weil's wind-down has pushed whole teams to rivals this month, and these 10 firms just announced moves like that. Fennemore is first because its merger with Gallagher & Kennedy goes live Dec. 1.

Method: pulled the last ~30 days of law firm merger, lateral and office-opening news from Google News, kept U.S. firms where a group of lawyers is changing firms or offices, and scored on how many timekeepers are moving and how soon. I did not check these against your current customers.

How it was built

  • Start with who actually buys from you, based on your own job post, site, and press, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days, plus a second signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on how recent the event is, how closely the account matches your buyer, and how usable it is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the saved source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add each firm's size from the Am Law and NLJ 500 lists, then build a rough "hours at risk" number per firm (new timekeepers times a typical capture gap) that an SE could use in the first call.

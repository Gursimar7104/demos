This page is a working IT helpdesk triage agent aimed at a Director of IT, and at the Entry-Level AI Automation Systems Specialist role: a staff ticket comes in, the agent classifies urgency and category, flags anything that looks like PHI or a HIPAA risk, drafts a fix or routes the ticket, and writes an audit trail. What this is for

A growing multi-site behavioral health provider cannot treat every IT request the same way. A printer jam can sit in L1. A Connect Hub outage at Union should page infrastructure. A request that names a client chart or a diagnosis should never be fulfilled from a general helpdesk queue. And the December 2026 outpatient opening needs a repeatable provisioning checklist (accounts, devices, EHR, Connect Hub) with an audit trail, not a loose email thread.

This prototype shows the shape of the work an AI Automation Systems Specialist could take on: classify, catch PHI, draft the next step, and leave a record. It is a demo, not a production system, and it is not connected to any live EHR, identity provider, or ticketing tool.

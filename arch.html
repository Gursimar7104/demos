Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 22, 2026, on real public data

Arch sells an AI growth platform to HVAC, plumbing, and electrical operators, especially PE roll-ups. I skipped Any Hour Group and Flint Group because Arch already names them as customers. What remains is a short list of other consolidators with a dated 2025-2026 close, recap, or named tuck-in, plus a first line you could send this week.

Method: DealSeam's HVAC PE roll-up tracker (updated Nov 18, 2026) plus the primary press links it cites. I did not use the TechCrunch software-funding feed here, because Arch's buyers are home-services platforms, not SaaS fundraises. Scores favor a close or add-on inside 2026, a clear official source, and a platform that still has to grow the brands it already owns.

How it was built

  • Start with a clear ICP from the company's own site or job post, not a generic employee-count filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated funding or capital event in the last ~90 days (or, for roll-up platforms, a dated 2025-2026 add-on or close), plus a second buyer signal.
  • Score 1-100 on recency, how tightly the account matches the ICP, and how usable the second signal is for a first line.
  • Every row has a source link. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add decision-maker names at the platform (Head of Growth / CMO), confirm which brands are in each new market, and drop a one-page campaign mock for the two highest-scoring accounts.

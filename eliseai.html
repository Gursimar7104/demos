Lead list for EliseAI · Revenue Operations Associate

10 apartment owners that just took on new units and need to lease them

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 24, 2026, on real public data

EliseAI handles tours, leasing questions, maintenance requests, and resident messages for property teams. The clearest moment to sell that is right after an owner buys a property, changes its manager, or plans a renovation, because leasing volume spikes and the team is not built out yet. These are multifamily deals from the last ~6 weeks.

Method: pulled Oct and Nov 2026 acquisitions from Multi-Housing News, Multifamily Dive, and Multifamily Executive, kept buyers that own and operate (not pure sellers), and scored on unit count, new markets, and renovation or rebrand plans. I did not check these against your current customers, and a few may already use EliseAI.

How it was built

  • Start with who actually buys from you, based on your own job post, site, and press, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days, plus a second signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on how recent the event is, how closely the account matches your buyer, and how usable it is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the saved source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add the management company for each deal (like Trinity on ReNew Brooklyn) as its own row, since they often pick the leasing software, and match each to your territories in Salesforce.

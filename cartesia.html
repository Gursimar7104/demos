How it works

  • Pull every open post from about 170 public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards (contact center, CX, healthcare, fintech, travel, telecom) through their public APIs.
  • Flag posts that mention voice agents, voice AI, TTS, STT, IVR or real-time voice, drop false hits like "voice of the customer", and drop companies already on Cartesia's customer page.
  • Keep each account's strongest post as the why-now link with a quote, score it with the rubric above, and add 2 entry roles and a one-line Cartesia angle.

What I'd improve next: Add named people for each entry role from Clay, pull voice launches and earnings-call mentions as a second signal, and re-run the board scan weekly so new voice hires bump an account up the list.

Target account list for Nash

Nash already names large logos such as Walmart and 7-Eleven. A GTM engineer still needs a mid-market list that is not those customers. Here are 10 public companies in commerce and delivery where last-mile complexity is already on the record.

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 2026

The GTM Engineer role is remote in the US and is hired to run agents and data behind pipeline. I excluded brands Nash already cites as customers or platform partners.

Nash’s site and job post describe last-mile orchestration across a company’s own fleets, partners, gig couriers, and stores. The job post names Walmart, Urban Outfitters, 7-Eleven, Woolworths, Shopify, and Toast. Those names are off this list. Sources: nash.ai and the GTM Engineer post.

Chipotle7High-volume off-premise orders; a classic “store employee plus courier” mix Nash mentions. Kept mid-list because of size.chipotle.com
Allbirds7DTC plus retail brand shipping physical goods, a Shopify-class merchant that is not the Shopify platform itself.allbirds.com
Warby Parker7Omnichannel retail with home try-on and store pickup, so delivery exceptions are a customer-trust issue.warbyparker.com

Publix8Regional grocer with pickup and delivery products, same last-mile pattern without being a named Nash customer.publix.com

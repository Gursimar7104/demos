I left Roku and Twitch off, because they are platforms, not long-form show teams, and I left a Social Media Editor title out of the Times count.

How it works

  • Start from media and podcast teams that make long-form shows and have public job boards.
  • Count video editor, video journalist, video producer and podcast-video titles in code, and leave out generic jobs that only mention the word video, plus stretch titles such as Social Media Editor.
  • A short why-today note is written from those titles, or from the posting when it is cited. A verifier checks every number and flags any claim the board does not support.

What I'd improve next: Add each company's YouTube or podcast catalog next, so a team with few job openings but a large long-form library still shows up.

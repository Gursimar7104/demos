9 school districts that just voted money for security

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 23, 2026, on real public data

Coram sells AI video security and gun detection to schools and other sites that already have cameras. The cleanest buying signal for a K-12 district is not a VC round, it is voters approving a bond or levy that names safety and security. These 9 districts all passed one on Nov 3, 2026, and each row says how much and what for.

Method: pulled Nov 3, 2026 school bond and levy results from local news, kept only measures that passed and name safety or security, and dropped ones that failed (McKinney Prop B) or were from older cycles. Chippewa Valley is the standout because its bond explicitly funds AI weapons detection. North Clackamas is a stretch and scored 60 so you can drop it.

How it was built

  • Start with the ICP from the company's own job post and site, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days (a raise, or a passed bond or levy), plus a second buyer signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on recency, how tightly the account matches the ICP, and how usable the signal is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the fetched source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add every Nov 2026 measure from state results feeds (Ohio, Texas, Michigan, Minnesota), parse the ballot language for "security", "camera", or "weapons", and join to districts that already run Verkada or Avigilon cameras from public board-meeting purchase approvals.

Fleet-change signal for PartsBase

Advanced Air got new FAA registrations for 12 turbine aircraft in 90 days, and 10 were built in 2006 or earlier

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 2026

Your post lists fleet changes and new repair station certifications as triggers. Older airframes run on aftermarket parts, so I ranked companies by older turbine aircraft that got a new FAA registration certificate in their name. From 1,502 company turbine registrations in the last 90 days, 12 owners added 3 or more aircraft built in 2006 or earlier.

How it works

  • Download the FAA's free registry file and keep turbine aircraft whose registration certificate was issued in the last 90 days to a company or LLC.
  • Remove manufacturers, banks and owner trusts, then count per owner how many added aircraft were built in 2006 or earlier.
  • Tag each owner as a likely buyer (operator adding an older fleet) or a likely supplier (parts companies buying airframes), ready to enrich in Clay. A new certificate can also mean a transfer, an N-number change or a renewal, so a seller confirms before calling.

What I'd improve next: Diff the registry file week over week so only true fleet additions count, add the FAA repair station certificates and the deregistration file, so a seller also sees new Part 145 shops and aircraft being retired for part-out, then push the top accounts and their buying committee into the CRM each Monday.

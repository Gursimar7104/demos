Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 25, 2026, on real public data

Enterpret sells to product and CX teams that have more customer feedback than they can read. Each company here just raised, launched, or changed owners this month, which usually means more users, more tickets and more reviews right away. Titan OS is the clearest case: 30 million users, up from 18 million last year, and new countries coming.

Method: pulled November funding and M&A news from TechCrunch and Google News, kept product-led companies where end users or customers generate a lot of feedback, and scored on how much new feedback the event creates and how close the company looks to your current customers. I did not check these against your customer list.

How it was built

  • Start with who actually buys from you, based on your own job post, site, and press, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days, plus a second signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on how recent the event is, how closely the account matches your buyer, and how usable it is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the saved source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add a second signal per account (app store review volume, new Head of Product Ops or Voice of Customer job posts) and route each one to the right rep with a first line that uses their own public reviews.

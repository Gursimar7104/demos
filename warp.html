9 newly funded startups about to hire, several in new states

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 24, 2026, on real public data

Warp runs payroll, HR, compliance, and benefits for high-growth companies, and the hard part it automates is hiring in a new state (registrations, tax accounts) or a new country. So the best accounts are startups that just raised and are about to hire, especially outside their home state. These all raised in the last ~6 weeks.

Method: took Oct and Nov 2026 rounds from TechCrunch's fundraising feed, kept early and growth-stage startups, then pulled each one's open roles from its public job board (Ashby or Lever) where I could find it. Rows with hiring in more than one state score highest. I did not check these against your current customers.

How it was built

  • Start with who actually buys from you, based on your own job post, site, and press, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days, plus a second signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on how recent the event is, how closely the account matches your buyer, and how usable it is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the saved source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Pull every open role for each account and flag the ones in a state the company has never hired in before. That is the exact moment Warp saves them work, and it would make a great trigger in Salesforce.

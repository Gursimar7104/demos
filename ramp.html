These 10 U.S. companies named a new CFO in November. Diversified is first because its CEO said out loud that the new CFO will modernize finance with AI and automation.

Method: pulled November CFO appointment press releases from Google News, kept U.S. mid-market and growth companies (no banks, no Fortune 100), and scored on how clearly the announcement points to finance changes (expansion, IPO prep, automation). How it was built

  • Start with who actually buys from you, based on your own job post, site, and press, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days, plus a second signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on how recent the event is, how closely the account matches your buyer, and how usable it is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the saved source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add a second signal per account (open AP or controller job posts, a recent raise) and auto-route each one into the right rep's book by segment and territory, which is the part of the job I would want to own.

LangChain's public Ashby board (102 jobs, including enterprise AE and renewals) is the hiring signal on the top row.

Method: GetLeads / Treg US funding feed (Nov 22) cleaned against TechCrunch links, Hone's seed blog, and LangChain and Trajectory Ashby boards checked the same day. I did not guess emails for these accounts. Rows are scored on how close they are to "an agent that needs to send mail," then marked down when the company is already very large.

How it was built

  • Start with a clear ICP from the company's own site or job post, not a generic employee-count filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated funding or capital event in the last ~90 days (or, for roll-up platforms, a dated 2025-2026 add-on or close), plus a second buyer signal.
  • Score 1-100 on recency, how tightly the account matches the ICP, and how usable the second signal is for a first line.
  • Every row has a source link. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add earlier-stage agent startups (seed / Series A) from a funding source that is not just TechCrunch mega-rounds, and attach a hiring or launch signal to each.

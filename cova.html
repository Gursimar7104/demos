Onboarding teardown for Cova

Cova is live in Michigan and says it handles the paperwork so families can get paid to give care. The official Home Help path still includes MiBridges, CHAMPS enrollment, a background check, and up to 45 days.

The Founding Growth Engineer role owns acquisition and the systems that turn a first conversation into an approved enrollment. I compared Cova’s public site with Michigan’s official Home Help page.

What Cova tells a family first

  • The homepage states Cova is live in Michigan and accepts most major insurance plans.
  • There are two entry paths, “For Caregivers” and “For Patients & Families,” both described as covered by Medicaid.
  • The caregiver path says Medicaid can pay you, Cova handles the paperwork, and training is free.
  • The first actions on the page are “Do I Qualify?”, “How It Works,” “Get started,” and “Contact a Care Guide.”

Source: cova.care.

What the official Michigan process still requires

  • Home Help is Michigan’s Independent Living program for in-home help.
  • If someone is not already on Medicaid, they apply at Michigan.gov/MiBridges or a local MDHHS office.
  • The Home Help application process can take up to 45 days.
  • Clients may choose a caregiver, but that person cannot be a spouse, and cannot be a parent caring for a minor child.
  • Caregivers must enroll in CHAMPS, pass a background check, and receive a provider ID before they can be paid.
  • Official forms listed on the page include DHS-390 (Adult Services Application) and MDHHS-6200 (Medical Needs Certification).
  • Caregivers are pointed to Michigan.gov/HomeHelp for CHAMPS instructions, or Provider Support at 800-979-4662.

Source: Michigan MDHHS, Independent Living (Home Help).

The growth implication

Paid and referral copy can stay simple (“get paid to care”), but the enrollment system the job wants to build should show a family where they are in those official steps: Medicaid status, medical-needs form, CHAMPS, provider ID, and the 45-day clock. That is the difference between a lead and an approved paycheck, and it is the part a growth engineer can instrument.

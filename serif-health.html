Serif turns price transparency files into usable negotiated-rate data for payers, providers, employers, and health tech. The best near-term buyers are companies that just raised and are now hiring someone whose job is payer contracts, payer performance, or reimbursement. Each row pairs a dated raise with that kind of opening.

Method: took Sept to Nov 2026 rounds from the Fierce Healthcare fundraising tracker and articles, checked each company's public Ashby or Greenhouse board for payer, partnership, RCM, or employer roles, and kept the ones with a real match. Wrong-board matches (a different "Otter" and a garage door "Rightway" on Greenhouse) were thrown out, so those two rows rely on the article only.

How it was built

  • Start with the ICP from the company's own job post and site, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days (a raise, or a passed bond or levy), plus a second buyer signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on recency, how tightly the account matches the ICP, and how usable the signal is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the fetched source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Run this weekly off a funding feed plus ATS checks, add a third signal from each company's own price-transparency or payer-network pages, and push matched rows into Serif's CRM with the opening as the first-line hook.

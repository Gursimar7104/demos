Pricing and docs teardown for AgentPhone

AgentPhone publishes a one-page agent rate card. A voice-AI buyer still has to assemble the same math from a general CPaaS price list.

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 2026

The Founding GTM Engineer role is about helping voice-AI and agent companies discover and adopt AgentPhone. I used only public pages to see how a first-time buyer would compare cost and setup.

This is not a missing-page audit. AgentPhone already ships docs, MCP support, and a public price list. The gap is how clearly that package is explained next to the tools buyers already know.

What the same buyer sees at a general CPaaS

Twilio publishes a US Programmable Voice pricing page, but the public HTML I loaded does not show a single starter bundle the way AgentPhone does. A buyer still has to add number rent, inbound and outbound minutes, messaging, and any AI add-ons from separate lists. Source: Twilio US Programmable Voice pricing and Twilio pricing hub.

Telnyx likewise sends buyers to a product and pricing catalog rather than one agent-sized monthly example. Source: telnyx.com/pricing.

What I would test next

A one-screen “first 30 days for a voice-agent team” calculator that starts from AgentPhone’s $404 example and asks the buyer to paste their current Twilio or Telnyx invoice lines. The Founding GTM role already owns experiments at the top of the funnel; this is one a developer buyer could finish without a call.

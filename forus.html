Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 23, 2026, on real public data

Forus gives every prescription an AI agent that clears insurance, financial assistance, and pharmacy steps, free for doctors and patients, and wants to be in every doctor's office in the US. Practices you can reach in batches are the leverage. These are care companies that raised in the last ~90 days and are adding prescribers or prescribing programs right now.

Method: took Sept to Nov 2026 rounds from the Fierce Healthcare fundraising tracker and articles, kept companies whose clinicians prescribe (or that route patients to prescriptions), and added a second signal from their job boards or announcements. Rightway and Healthleap are partner-style rows, scored lower so you can cut them.

How it was built

  • Start with the ICP from the company's own job post and site, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days (a raise, or a passed bond or levy), plus a second buyer signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on recency, how tightly the account matches the ICP, and how usable the signal is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the fetched source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add a specialty filter that mirrors where Forus is strongest (dermatology first, GI after the AGA partnership, then rheumatology), and pull new independent specialty groups from state license and NPI registrations alongside funding news.

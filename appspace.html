9 companies moving or growing headquarters, with a workplace to set up

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 24, 2026, on real public data

Appspace sells the workplace layer: intranet, digital signage, desk and room booking, and visitor management. Those projects get budget when a company moves its HQ, shrinks its footprint, or packs a lot of new hires into one site. These are U.S. HQ moves and expansions announced in the last ~90 days.

Method: pulled corporate HQ and office announcements from Area Development and local business news, kept U.S. companies with a large office or frontline workforce, and scored on headcount, how soon the move happens, and whether space is shrinking. I did not check these against your current customers.

How it was built

  • Start with who actually buys from you, based on your own job post, site, and press, not a generic firmographic filter.
  • Keep only accounts with a dated, sourced event in the last ~90 days, plus a second signal where one exists.
  • Score 1-100 on how recent the event is, how closely the account matches your buyer, and how usable it is for a first line.
  • Every number on this page was checked in code against the saved source text. Nothing here is invented.

What I'd improve next: Add a second signal per account (a new Head of Workplace hire, an RFP for signage, or a return-to-office announcement) and build each one a short demo storyline using their own new office.

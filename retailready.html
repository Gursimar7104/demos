Site and competitor-page gap check for RetailReady

RetailReady's sitemap has 12 marketing URLs and 0 comparison or alternatives pages, even though the BDR role will be talking to brands that already know SPS Commerce, TrueCommerce, and CommerceHub.

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 2026

Your public site already explains EDI, packout, and chargeback disputes, and it names the job: help brands ship right the first time and win money back when a chargeback slips through. A first conversation is easier when there is a page that puts RetailReady next to the tools those brands already have.

What the sitemap includes

12 URLs: the homepage, search, EDI, packout, chargebacks, about, privacy, terms, and a few related brand or 3PL pages. No /vs, /compare, or /alternatives path.

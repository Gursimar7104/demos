Score (out of 100)

  • Signal type, 30: a new owner of internal AI (chief AI officer, AI enablement, AI governance) 30; security or GRC leadership 20; funding round only 12.
  • Vertical, 25: healthcare, financial services, legal and accounting score in full, since those are the three verticals on z0.ai's site.
  • Size, 20: 200 to 5,000 employees scores in full. Smaller companies are Team-tier pilots, and bigger ones are Enterprise.
  • Recency, 15: under 30 days 15, under 60 days 10, under 90 days 5.
  • Reachability, 10: a named buyer or a clear reporting line in the post, NYC presence, and a team that already works in Slack.

The pipeline I'd build for z0.ai

  1. Sources (daily, n8n cron): Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever job APIs for a watchlist of regulated mid-market companies, filtered by title regex (AI enablement, AI governance, CISO, GRC, IT director). Add press feeds (Law360 Pulse, NJBIZ, Behavioral Health Business, FinTech Global) and a funding feed for new rounds. New CISO and chief AI officer announcements go straight to the top.
  2. Enrich: domain to headcount, industry and HQ. Find the named buyer from the post's reporting line or the press release, then a work email that's verified before it counts. Flag Slack usage from job posts that mention Slack.
  3. Score: the rubric above, written in Python so every number can be traced back to the source text. Anything under 60 is held for review.
  4. HubSpot sync: upsert the company on its domain with custom properties for signal_type, signal_date, signal_url and score, then the contact with its verification status. Dedupe on domain plus person, and add a task for the founder when the score is 90 or more.
  5. Sequence: 3 touches over 10 days. The first line quotes the signal, for example "saw you named your first chief AI officer". A/B test the subject line, and a reply sends a Slack alert and stops the sequence.
  6. Dashboard (Postgres): signals found per week, by source, mapped to replies and booked demos. Sources that don't convert get cut.

What I'd automate first at a 5-person company: the daily signal pull and HubSpot upsert, so the founder starts each morning with 5 scored accounts and the reason each one is worth an email today. Next I'd add a check that every claim in the first line matches the source text, because a wrong fact in an email to a CISO costs more than a missed send.

Comparison page gap finder for Confident AI

6 of 10 eval competitors have no Confident AI comparison page. Galileo is one, and 5 rival sites already target it.

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 2026

Your role lists SEO/AEO and programmatic pages, so I diffed your sitemap (1106 URLs) against sitemaps from 12 vendor sites, the 10 below plus Helicone and Patronus (17,835 URLs). You already have strong vs/alternatives pages for Langfuse, LangSmith, Braintrust, Arize Phoenix. The gaps: Galileo, Ragas, Promptfoo, Humanloop, Opik (Comet), W&B Weave. 6 of the 10 changed owners or shut down since 2025, and 4 of those (Galileo, Promptfoo, Humanloop and W&B Weave) have users with a reason to switch and no Confident AI page to land on. Meanwhile Braintrust runs 3 pages aimed at Confident AI and DeepEval.

How it works

  • Pull confident-ai.com/sitemap.xml and the sitemaps listed in each competitor's robots.txt, 17,835 URLs in total, and check llms.txt on each domain.
  • Match comparison URLs by slug (vs, compare, alternatives, competitors) per competitor, then count how many rival sites already publish a page on that competitor and which pages target Confident AI or DeepEval.
  • Rank gaps by contest and buying intent: tools that were acquired or sunset (Galileo, Humanloop, Promptfoo, W&B) give their users a reason to compare, so those pages go first. The same script reruns weekly as a diff.

What I'd improve next: Join Search Console clicks and AI-answer citations (Perplexity, ChatGPT search) to each gap so pages ship in order of real query volume, generate drafts from DeepEval docs plus a fixed template, and track sign-ups per page in the attribution layer.

Target account list for RamAIn

RamAIn trains computer-use agents to work inside the same desktops and portals a new hire would use. Here are 10 real organizations where that pain is already public.

Built by Gursimar Singh, Nov 2026

The Founding GTM Engineer job is the first business hire and owns ICP scoring and outbound. I only kept accounts with a public source for the reason.

RamAIn’s public site and YC page describe AI employees that operate legacy systems, desktop apps, and web portals the way a person would. Sources: ramain.ai, YC company page.

How I would use this

Start outbound with public-benefit portals like Home Help and one auto-retail roof, because both have written, checkable steps. Then add Clay scoring on public mentions of CHAMPS, DMS, dispatch, or work-order systems.
